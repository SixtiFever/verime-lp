Caller Verification

Restore Trust in Outbound Calls

VeriMe delivers live, in-call proof of identity — authenticated by your identity provider — so customers know it’s really you calling.

The Problem

  • Rising Fraud: Phone scams are increasing in number and sophistication.
  • Brand Damage: Scammers impersonate trusted organisations to con the public.
  • Dead Channel: Customers no longer answer because phone communication is broken.

The Solution

VeriMe is a caller verification system that delivers live, in-call proof of identity to call receivers. Authenticated by your identity provider, it ensures only genuine employees can verify themselves, completely shutting out scammers.

How It Works

1

Connect

Connect your identity provider to VeriMe once.

2

Call

Call customers or residents as normal.

3

Verify

Send live, secure proof of identity mid-call.

4

Confirm

Receivers instantly view your name, role, and company via the VeriMe app.
